Help Center
How do I create an account?
Click "Register" on the top right, fill in your details, and verify your email address. It's free and takes less than a minute!
How do I post a vehicle for sale?
Once logged in, click "Post Ad" in the navigation menu. Fill in your vehicle details, upload clear photos, and publish your listing. All listings are reviewed within 24 hours.
What does "Verified Seller" mean?
Verified Sellers have submitted valid identification documents that we've reviewed and approved. This badge builds trust and helps buyers feel confident.
Is it free to list a vehicle?
Yes! Listing vehicles on Chrandi Motors is completely free. There are no hidden fees or commissions.
How do I stay safe when buying a car?
Always meet in a public place, inspect the vehicle thoroughly, test drive before purchasing, and never send money without seeing the vehicle first.
How do I edit or delete my listing?
Go to "My Ads" in your profile, find the listing, and click the edit or delete button.
How are ratings calculated?
Ratings are based on reviews from verified buyers. The average rating is displayed on seller profiles.
